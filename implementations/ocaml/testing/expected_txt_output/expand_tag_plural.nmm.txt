§ 2    Our main results
       ────────────────

¶ 2.1  THEOREMS (Main)

       (1)   Nothing lasts forever.

       (2)   All is well iff

             (a)   nothing is bad, and

             (b)   I know that '[ITM:2+]' is rendered nicely, like
                   this: (+).

             (+)   I know that '[ITM:2a]' is rendered nicely, like
                   this: (a).

       (X)   '[ITM:2a]' -> (2)(a)

       ─     '[ITM:2+]' -> (2)(+)

       '[ITM:2a]' -> (2)(a)

       '[ITM:2+]' -> (2)(+)

       '[ITM:1]' -> THEOREM (1)

       '[ITM:X]' -> THEOREM (X)


¶ 2.2  PROOF (of THEOREMS 2.1)  By straightforward induction on the
       complexity of nothing.
