§ 1

¶ 1.1  THEOREM (Main)

       (1)   Nothing lasts forever.

       (2)   All is well.

             (a)   sometimes

             (b)   forever

       '[ITM:1]' -> (1)

       '[ITM:2]' -> (2)

       '[ITM:2a]' -> (2)(a)

       '[ITM:2b]' -> (2)(b)


¶ 1.2  PROOF (of THEOREM 1.1)  By straightforward induction on the
       complexity of nothing.

       '[THM:main]' -> THEOREM 1.1

       '[ITM:1]' -> 1.1(1)

       '[ITM:2]' -> 1.1(2)

       '[ITM:2a]' -> 1.1(2)(a)

       '[ITM:2b]' -> 1.1(2)(b)
